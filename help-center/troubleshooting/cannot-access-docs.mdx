title: Cannot Access My Documentation
description: Fix access problems in Shalostx.
---

## Issue

- Documentation pages are not loading.
- You see an "Access Denied" error.

## Causes

- Not signed in
- Missing permissions

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Login Status">
    ````
    Make sure you are signed in to your Shalostx account.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Permissions">
    ````
    Ask your project admin to verify your access.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
